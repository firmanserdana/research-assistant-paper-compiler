## Stimulating Peripheral Nerve to Enable Hand And/or Finger Control (1 papers)

### A closed-loop hand prosthesis with simultaneous intraneural tactile and position feedback
**Authors:** Edoardo D’Anna; Giacomo Valle; Alberto Mazzoni; Ivo Strauss; Francesco Iberite; Jérémy Patton; Francesco M. Petrini; Stanisa Raspopovic; Giuseppe Granata; Riccardo Di Iorio; Marco Controzzi; Christian Cipriani; Thomas Stieglitz; Paolo M. Rossini; Silvestro Micera
**DOI:** 10.1126/scirobotics.aau8892
**TRL:** 5
**Keywords:** intraneural stimulation, tactile feedback, proprioception, bionic hand, sensory substitution
**Summary:** This paper introduces a **closed-loop hand prosthesis** for transradial amputees that simultaneously delivers **somatotopic intraneural tactile feedback** via implanted electrodes and **proprioceptive position feedback** through sensory substitution, enabling real-time joint angle reproduction (median 9.1° precision) and object discrimination (75.5% accuracy) comparable to healthy individuals. In trials with two amputees, this multimodal approach reduced reliance on visual cues, improved task performance without vision, and supported discrimination of object size and compliance. For **biorobotics research**, it advances bidirectional neural interfaces by demonstrating scalable, efficient integration of rich sensory cues, paving the way for sophisticated bionic limbs with enhanced embodiment and functionality.
