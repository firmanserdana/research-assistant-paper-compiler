## EMG Decoding on Spinal Cord Injured Patients (2 papers)

### Surface EMG in Subacute and Chronic Care after Traumatic Spinal Cord Injuries
**Authors:** Gustavo Balbinot
**DOI:** 10.3390/traumacare2020031
**TRL:** 3
**Keywords:** Surface EMG, spinal cord injury, motor recovery, neurorehabilitation, neurophysiological assessment
**Summary:** Examines the clinical utility of surface electromyography (sEMG) in subacute and chronic care following traumatic spinal cord injury to quantify residual motor command, detect subclinical muscle activation below the lesion level, and guide targeted neurorehabilitation and neuroprosthetic interventions.

### Effectiveness Of Robotic Assisted Gait Training In Spinal Cord Injured Patients – A Literature Review
**Authors:** Anlitta C Johnson; Nischitha R Rao; Sreshtta Tinson
**DOI:** 10.55248/gengpi.6.0625.23105
**TRL:** 4
**Keywords:** Robotic assisted gait training, exoskeleton rehabilitation, spinal cord injury, locomotor recovery
**Summary:** Literature review evaluating robotic-assisted gait training (RAGT) and lower-limb exoskeleton systems in spinal cord injured patients, analyzing biomechanical outcomes, muscle activation patterns, walking independence, and neuroplastic motor recovery compared to conventional overground physical therapy.
